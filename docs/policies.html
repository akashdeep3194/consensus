Privacy Policy

The short version, true today: signing in needs a Google account; we store the email and a display handle that comes with it, plus your predictions and votes. We don't run ads, don't use analytics or tracking scripts, and don't sell data to anyone. Full detail below.

01What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to authenticate you and run the game — nothing beyond that is gathered anywhere in the product.

Google account ID
A stable identifier from Google Sign-In, so we recognise you on return visits.
Email address
Provided by Google at sign-in. Stored, never shown publicly (see below).
Display handle
Suggested from your Google name, editable by you. Shown publicly.
Gameplay activity
Your predictions, votes, points and streaks, tied to your account.
Passwords
Never collected — sign-in is Google-only, nothing to store or leak.
Payment information
The game has no purchases, so none is ever requested.
Precise location
Not requested and not read from your device or browser.
Analytics or ad trackers
No analytics SDK, ad pixel or third-party tracker is loaded anywhere in the app.

One third-party request happens on every page load regardless of sign-in: the page's typeface is served from Google Fonts, which — like any font-hosting CDN — sees the requesting IP address as part of serving the file. No account information is attached to that request.

02Why we collect it

Every field above exists to do one of two things: prove who you are (the Google ID and email) or run the game you're playing (your handle, predictions, votes, and score). We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating Consensus 3D.

04What's shown publicly

Your handle and your gameplay results (predictions, tier, score, rank) appear on public leaderboards and result boards — that public record is the point of the game. Your email address is never displayed anywhere, to any other player, at any time.

05Cookies

We set exactly two, both first-party and neither used for tracking or advertising:

  • A session cookie — cryptographically signed proof you're signed in. HTTP-only (invisible to page scripts), and required for the game to know who you are.
  • A short-lived sign-in state cookie — exists only during the few seconds of the Google sign-in handshake, to prevent a forged login request; deleted immediately after.
